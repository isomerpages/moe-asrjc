<h1><strong>ASRJC as the 6th CLEP JC Centre</strong></h1>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/CLEP/ASR_CLEP_Publicity_Poster_2026_Landscape.png">
</div>
<p><strong>Where Passion Meets Excellence — Join our legacy to deepen your love and mastery of Chinese language and literature!</strong>
</p>
<p>The Chinese Language Elective Programme (CLEP) at Anderson Serangoon Junior
College (ASRJC) will officially launch in 2027!</p>
<p>Minister for Education Desmond Lee made the official announcement on 1
August 2026 at the opening ceremony of the Mother Tongue Languages Symposium,
declaring ASRJC as the sixth junior college in Singapore to offer the CLEP.
ASRJC will roll out a rich and vibrant range of Chinese language and literature
learning programmes, dedicated to creating a platform for students to showcase
their talents!</p>
<p>We look forward to welcoming students passionate about Chinese language
and literature to join us as ASRJC’s inaugural CLEP cohort, as we embark
on this exciting new chapter together!</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>启航 2027！安德逊实龙岗初级学院正式获选开办“华文语文特选课程”</strong>
</p>
<p>安德逊实龙岗初级学院（ASRJC）“华文语文特选课程”（简称“语特”）将于 <strong>2027 年</strong>正式启航！</p>
<p>教育部长李智陞于 8 月 1 日在母语学习论坛开幕典礼上正式宣布，我院获选为新加坡第六所开办语特的初级学院。ASRJC 将推出精彩多元的华文与文学学习项目，倾心为学生搭建展示才华的舞台！</p>
<p>期待更多热爱华文与文学的同学成为 ASRJC 语特首届学员，与我们一同开启崭新篇章！</p>
